# Zalvantic FAQ

## How long does a typical project take?

Most projects take 2 to 8 weeks depending on complexity. A landing page or branding project can be delivered in 1 to 2 weeks. A full custom software or AI integration project usually takes 4 to 8 weeks. A clear timeline is given during the initial consultation.

## What is the pricing structure?

Zalvantic uses project-based pricing that depends on the scope of work. After a discovery call, the client receives a transparent proposal with no hidden fees. Zalvantic serves clients in Pakistan, the Gulf region and worldwide.

## Is ongoing maintenance and support available?

Yes. Zalvantic offers flexible retainer packages for post-launch support, feature updates, performance monitoring and AI model maintenance. Most clients choose an ongoing partnership for continuous improvement.

## Can AI be integrated into an existing product?

Yes. Zalvantic specialises in AI integration, including LLMs, chatbots, automation pipelines and ML-powered features, on existing platforms regardless of tech stack. The team starts with a technical audit and then proposes the most efficient integration path.

## What technologies does Zalvantic use?

The stack includes Python, Flask, Django, React, Next.js, Node.js, PostgreSQL, MySQL and Docker, plus AI frameworks such as LangChain, OpenAI and local LLMs. Tools are chosen to fit each project's requirements.

## Does Zalvantic work with international clients?

Yes. Zalvantic works with clients in Pakistan, Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar and globally. The team works async-first, with clear communication cadences and timezone flexibility.
